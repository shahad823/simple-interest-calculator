# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards
Treat others with empathy and respect. Welcome different viewpoints, give constructive feedback, accept responsibility for mistakes, and focus on the community. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers clarify and enforce these standards fairly. They may remove or edit comments, commits, issues, and contributions that violate this code, and explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope
This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public, including discussions, issues, pull requests, and events.

## Enforcement
Report unacceptable behavior to the repository owner, shahad823, through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Do not include private or sensitive incident details in public issues. Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, respect reporter privacy, and avoid retaliation.

## Enforcement Guidelines
### 1. Correction
For an isolated minor violation, maintainers provide a warning explaining the behavior and the expected correction.

### 2. Warning
For a serious or repeated violation, maintainers issue a warning with consequences and may restrict interactions for a specified period.

### 3. Temporary Ban
For sustained inappropriate behavior, maintainers temporarily prohibit participation and contact with affected community members for a specified period.

### 4. Permanent Ban
For persistent violations, harassment, threats, or aggression, maintainers permanently prohibit participation in project spaces.
